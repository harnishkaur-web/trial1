Ten Starter Prompts

Ready-to-use prompts for your first time working with an approved AI tool — for study, work and home.

Your first prompt does not need to be clever. It needs to be clear, safe, and checked before you use the answer.

Use an approved toolOnly send these prompts through your institution's approved AI tool and account.

Replace the bracketsEvery [bracket] is a placeholder. Swap it for your own real task, never personal data.
