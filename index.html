Before you start

Three simple rules

Keep these in mind every time you use a prompt.

Use an approved tool

Only use the AI tool and account your institute allows. Not a random app.

Keep personal details out

Never type your name, ID number, address or photos into a prompt.

Read the whole answer

An AI answer is a first draft. Check it before you copy, send or use it.

Example

What an answer can look like

You ask
Explain photosynthesis in simple words, as if to a first-year student. Use one everyday example. Keep it under 150 words.
A possible AI answer
“Photosynthesis is how plants make their own food. Think of a leaf as a tiny kitchen. Sunlight is the stove. Water and carbon dioxide are the ingredients. The leaf cooks them into sugar for energy, and gives off oxygen as a bonus.”
This is only an example. Always read your own AI's answer in full, and check any facts before you use it.
