Ten Starter Prompts

Ready-to-use AI prompts for study, work and home — swap the [bracket], read every answer before you use it.

Study
1

Explain [topic name] in simple terms, as if to a first-year student, using one everyday example. Keep it to 150 words.

2

Turn these notes into 5 short revision points I can read before a test: [paste your notes here].

3

I have an assignment due on [date] about [topic]. Suggest a simple structure with headings, and one question to answer under each heading.

Work
4

Write a short reminder for my batch about [practical session, e.g. tool return]. No more than 3 lines, in plain language.

5

Here is my workshop inventory list. Point out anything missing or unclear, and suggest a simple format to record it: [paste your list].

6

Draft a polite email to my [facilitator / faculty title] asking for [a short extension / a clarification] on [assignment name]. Keep it under 80 words.

7

Make a checklist for my project on [project topic], from first draft to submission on [date]. No more than 8 items.

Home
8

Suggest a simple one-week plan to practise [subject or skill], with one rest day and no more than one hour a day.

9

Turn these notes into a short checklist for [event, e.g. a study group meet-up]: [paste your notes].

10

Summarise this in 3 plain-English bullet points: [paste any short text].

Before you use it: read the whole answer · check names, dates & facts · AI supports your work, it doesn't replace your teacher or facilitator.
